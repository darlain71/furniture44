EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Everything you need to know about our craftsmanship, ordering process, and delivery.

Yes, we specialize in custom-built furniture tailored to your exact specifications. Whether you need a specific dimension for a dining table or a custom pulpit design for your sanctuary, our master craftsmen will work with you to bring your vision to life.

We care deeply about our environmental footprint. All our wood is sourced from responsibly managed, sustainable forests under accredited international certification, ensuring both premium quality and environmental conservation.

Typically, custom pieces take 2 to 4 weeks depending on the complexity of the design and our current workshop schedule. Once completed, standard delivery takes 3-4 working days.

Yes, we offer reliable delivery and professional setup services across the region. Our team ensures that your furniture is transported safely and assembled perfectly in your designated space.

We offer flexible payment options. Typically, we require a deposit to initiate custom orders, with the balance payable upon delivery or according to agreed terms. We also support secure mobile money and bank transfers.
